The preserved norberto-dragon.glb file is the original downloaded model, with its
embedded artist, source, license, six texture images and Object_0 animation intact.
The source was confirmed against the public Sketchfab model API on 2026-09-20.

Changes in Dragonfall's JavaScript adapter, not in the GLB:
- Uniform scale and 180-degree heading rotation to match the game coordinates.
- Original morph-target flight animation is retained.
- Narrow-cave glide blends to the artist's original animation frame at 35/30
  seconds, then blends back to the original flight clip after the cave.
- Mouth, reins and wing-tip positions follow the animated mesh vertices.
- The model's authored rider is hidden in first-person view to avoid covering
  Dragonfall's rider camera. It remains visible in chase view.
- Optional earned colors, horns and saddle decorations; the initial appearance
  keeps the original dragon materials and has no added decorations.

No endorsement by the artist is implied.
